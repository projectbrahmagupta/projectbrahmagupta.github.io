\begin{problem}{Sporadic Sequences}
Ramanujan--Sato series generalise Ramanujan's formulas for \(1/\pi\) by replacing the binomial coefficients in the summand with other sequences of integers. The sequences that work satisfy an Apéry-like recurrence, and integrality is a severe restriction: the recurrence divides by \((n+1)^2\) at every step, so almost every choice of parameters produces a fraction within a few terms.

For integers \(a,b,c\) define \(u_{-1}=0\), \(u_0=1\), and
    \[
    (n+1)^2u_{n+1}=(an^2+an+b)u_n-cn^2u_{n-1},
    \qquad n\ge0.
    \]
Call the triple \((a,b,c)\) integral if \(u_0,u_1,\dots,u_{108}\) are all integers. How many integral triples satisfy \(|a|,|b|,|c|\le108\)?
\end{problem}
