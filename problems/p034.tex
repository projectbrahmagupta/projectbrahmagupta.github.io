\begin{problem}{The End Correction}
Nīlakaṇṭha transmits three correction terms attributed to Mādhava, applied after truncating the series for \(\pi/4\) at \(n\) terms:
    \[
    \frac{\pi}{4}\approx
    1-\frac13+\frac15-\cdots
    +\frac{(-1)^{n-1}}{2n-1}
    +(-1)^nF(n),
    \]
    with
    \[
    F_1(n)=\frac1{4n},\qquad
    F_2(n)=\frac{n}{4n^2+1},\qquad
    F_3(n)=\frac{n^2+1}{4n^3+5n}.
    \] 
For each of the three, let \(n_i\) be the least \(n\) for which the corrected sum differs from \(\pi/4\) by less than \(10^{-12}\). Find \(n_1+n_2+n_3\).
\end{problem}
