\begin{problem}{Ardhaccheda of a Tower}
Jaina texts define the \emph{ardhaccheda} of a positive integer as the number of times it can be halved, and state that the ardhaccheda of a product is the sum of the ardhacchedas of its factors. The same literature builds very large numbers by the operation \emph{vargita-saṃvargita}, which sends \(x\) to \(x^x\).
    
Let \(x_1=96\) and \(x_{k+1}=x_k^{\,x_k}\). Find the ardhaccheda of \(x_4\), modulo \(10^9+7\).
\end{problem}
